About screening

Understanding
your screening.

What does my result mean?

Your full screening returns “Research screening positive” or “Research screening negative” using an app threshold of 18.18 out of 100. These categories describe model output; they do not confirm or rule out TB. Complete the questionnaire after the initial cough and pulse analysis to obtain your full screening result.

Why is the app threshold 18.18?

It is the next higher calibration score above the previous 13.19 threshold. On the same 188-person calibration set, false positives fell from 87 to 56, while missed TB cases rose from 3 to 7. These are calibration observations, not validated phone-app performance.

Is the camera reading a medical measurement?

The camera estimates pulse from color changes in your fingertip. We wait for a stable signal, but readings have not been validated on your phone. It does not measure blood pressure or oxygen saturation.

What happens to my recording?

Audio is sent to the project’s local laptop for processing. Recordings and answers are not written to files. A small audio-embedding cache stays in server memory until the process stops. You choose whether to download a summary.

What should I do after screening?

This is an initial research screening, not a diagnosis. A positive result requires clinical assessment and further testing. A negative result cannot rule out TB. A healthcare professional may arrange a sputum molecular test and other investigations. Seek assessment for persistent cough, fever, night sweats, or unexplained weight loss, regardless of the score.

Further testing guidance: WHO: TB screening. This research app is not a WHO-recommended screening test.
